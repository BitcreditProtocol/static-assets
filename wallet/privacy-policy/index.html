Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-02-13

This Privacy Policy describes how Bitcredit Wallet (“the App”) handles information.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Camera Permission

The App requests access to the device camera only to scan QR codes (for example, to scan wallet addresses or payment requests).

  • Camera access is used only when you choose to scan a QR code.
  • The App does not take photos, record video, or store camera images.
  • QR code scanning is processed on your device and is not sent to us.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties.

Data Retention

Since we do not collect personal information, we do not retain it.

Security

We design the App to minimize data handling. Any QR scanning happens locally on your device.

Changes to This Policy

If we change this Privacy Policy, we will update the “Last updated” date above.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: github@bitcr.org
